How to use this calculator

This HMI turns your BOM into a fabrication cost & quotation. Work top to bottom:

1 · Customer & Order

  • Fill company, contact, phone — they appear on the quotation and WhatsApp message.
  • MOQ / Order Qty decides the bend & weld slab rate (below 100 vs 100+).

2 · Rates & Materials

  • Material Master: ₹/kg and density per type (MS CRCA, HRPO, SS 304/202, GI, GP). Add your own.
  • Rate Master: laser ₹/m, pierce, bend, weld, powder-coat, scrap %.
  • Raw Material Store: every sheet size/thickness you stock, with quantity. Nesting auto-picks the least-wastage sheet and flags shortages.

3 · Parts

  • + Parent / + Child to add components; tick processes (laser, bend, weld, powder coat).
  • Or bulk-upload DXF files (dimensions & cut length auto-read) or import a BOM Excel (download the template first).

4 · Tubes & Sections

  • Square (40×40, 50×50), rectangular or round — enter size, wall thickness, length, qty and material. Weight & cost are automatic.

5 · Interactive Nesting Board

  • DXF parts appear in the palette with their true outer profile & inner contours (units auto-converted to mm). Drag them onto the sheet — they seat with your chosen spacing (e.g. 2 or 5 mm) and rotate in 15°/45°/90° steps.
  • ⚡ Auto Nest packs everything for you. Download the result as a nested .DXF (straight to the laser) or .PDF.

6 · Review & Output

  • Check weights: net parts, sheets used, and unutilised wastage.
  • Quotation (Print/PDF) — customer-ready document. Excel — full workbook with live formulas. Save to Drive — pushes everything to your Google Sheet. WhatsApp/Email — send instantly.
